# Notation index

This index reproduces Appendix L of the [canonical manuscript](manuscript.md). It introduces no additional assumptions or definitions. Chiral labels refer to the two spin factors in the fixed convention, and the numerical Einstein label is always the one in $\operatorname{Ric}=\Lambda g$. The Lorentz signature is $(+,-,-,-)$; the curvature contraction and primary printed-label dictionary are collected in §2.
