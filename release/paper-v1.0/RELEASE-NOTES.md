proof note's v0.2.7 import provenance is retained.

preserved. The four main theorem blocks, rendered references, contact footnote and

comparisons and the specified piecewise optical model retain their scope.

export profile and the retained export-only layout adjustments.

edition. New mathematical results are not introduced by the version label.

and SOURCE-CHECK.json.
